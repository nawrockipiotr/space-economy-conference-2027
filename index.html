Why Join?

Why Join?

The conference is designed for scholars and practitioners who want to help shape the management research agenda for the Space Economy. Participants will have opportunities to:

Present and develop research
Receive feedback from senior faculty and discussants
Engage with industry professionals
Build new research collaborations
Explore publication pathways
Connect with the growing Space Economy research community
Track one

Academic track

Invitations will be extended to authors of selected submissions and senior faculty discussants. In the first stage, please submit an extended abstract of no more than 500 words presenting a clear and compelling research idea related to the Space Economy.

→Submissions may be conceptual, empirical, phenomenon-driven, or methodological
→Work in progress is welcome, provided that the submission has a sufficiently developed research question, argument, or empirical direction
→It is our intention to develop proposals for special issues in major FT50 and or industry-/policy-facing journals by the time of the conference
Read the Academic Track
Track two

Professional track

The Professional Track creates opportunities for researchers to engage directly with industry professionals and practitioners working across the Space Economy. In the first stage, please submit a proposal abstract of no more than 500 words that identifies a specific managerial or organisational challenge in the space sector and presents a distinctive solution or approach to addressing it.

→Participate in dedicated sessions with industry professionals
→Join a “speed dating” lunch bringing practitioners and academics together
→Engage in parallel industry-facing activities
→Participate in the Shark Tank Finale, where venture capital investors and leading academics will evaluate selected pitches and provide exposure to investors
