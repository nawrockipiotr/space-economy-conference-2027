Submission

We invite both management scholars and Space Economy professionals to submit to the conference. In Stage 1, participants are invited to submit an extended abstract. Selected authors will then be invited to present their work at the conference and receive developmental feedback from our academic and professional committees.

Extended Abstract Submission Deadline30 October 2026
Full Paper Submission Deadline (for accepted academic abstracts)15 January 2027
Submission portalcmt3.research.microsoft.com/SEC2027
Submit an Abstract

Two-stage submission process

In Stage 1, participants submit an extended abstract. Selected submissions will be invited to present at the conference and receive developmental feedback. For Stage 2, accepted authors in the Academic Track will be expected to submit their full papers, while accepted participants in the Professional Track will be expected to submit their presentation slides.
